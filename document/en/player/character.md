
# character
Review and manipulation of the protagonist's situation
- This content is identical to what is displayed by manipulating the Category.
___

## activity
List of All Actions
- A long press toggles the execution of the Action.
___

## inventory
List of All Items
- Press and hold to toggle Equipment of Item.
___

## status
List of all properties
- Only the value of the correction of the Category by Item is shown.
- There are further other corrections depending on the Action to be performed.
___

## Mission
List of All Tasks
- The Tasks are grouped and displayed by Category.
- The Tasks with no Category configured are grouped together at the top.
- A check mark is shown on the Tasks that have been completed.
- A ribbon marks the Tasks whose reward has not been claimed yet. Select one and press claim to receive it.
- Once a Task has been completed it stays completed, even if the conditions are no longer met afterwards.
