
# character
Review and manipulation of the protagonist's situation
- This content is identical to what is displayed by manipulating the Category.
___

## activity
List of All Actions
- A long press toggles the execution of the Action.
___

## inventory
List of All Items
- Press and hold to toggle Equipment of Item.
___

## status
List of all properties
- Only the value of the correction of the Category by Item is shown.
- There are further other corrections depending on the Action to be performed.
___

## Mission
List of All Tasks
- They are grouped and displayed by category.
- Tasks without a category are grouped together and displayed at the top.
- Completed tasks are marked with a check mark.
- Tasks for which you haven't yet received your reward will be marked with a ribbon. Select the task and tap "Claim" to receive your reward.
- Tasks that no longer meet the conditions after being completed will have a line through their icon and will revert to "Uncompleted" status.
